**Authority:** [`EMBER_ASH_CANONICAL_VISION.md`](EMBER_ASH_CANONICAL_VISION.md) (v1.3) governs. This register records what was
observed, what the code does now and what decision is pending. It approves nothing: an observation listed here is **not** an
approved gameplay change, and no entry authorises implementation. Companion documents:
[`EMBER_ASH_DECISION_REGISTER.md`](EMBER_ASH_DECISION_REGISTER.md) and [`EMBER_ASH_ROADMAP_REGISTER.md`](EMBER_ASH_ROADMAP_REGISTER.md).

## How to read it

- **Scope**, **Delivery** and **Target** use the decision register's vocabulary (see its §0). For observations 1–9, Scope
  `APPROVED` means "authorised and delivered in Milestone A2". For 11–21, `APPROVED` is used only where the observation
  reports a defect in behaviour that is already approved, or where canonical text explicitly requires the behaviour (quoted);
  even then **implementation is not authorised**. Everything else the user raised is `PROPOSED`; observation 10 is
  `UNDECIDED` (design discussion).
- Proposed observations carry Target `BACKLOG`; a likely gate is noted as a candidate only, never as a schedule.
- Delivery for 1–9 was verified on the integrated development branch: suite 283/283, unit 384/384, compile 221 scripts
  (Run 0, `fa7b5b7`); rendered phases `a2_experience`, `a2econ`, `consult_rl` and the 48-phase regression passed on A2's
  head `e045d67` (identical tree). Human Windows gameplay acceptance of A2 is still outstanding (CV §11) and is not
  repeated per row.
